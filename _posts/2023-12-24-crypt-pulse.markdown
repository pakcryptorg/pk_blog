## April 2019 – QKD “Unhackable” Crypto Hype vs Reality
Systems fail in the messy interfaces (implementations, side channels, key management), long before the underlying math gives way. In 2019 the hype increasingly referenced quantum key distribution (QKD) boxes and national quantum initiatives. QKD does offer information theoretic key exchange, but scaling, integration, and endpoint compromise still bite. Real-world security posture depends more on patch discipline, correct primitives, and eliminating downgrade/side channel surfaces than on buying “unhackable” gadgets. The takeaway for practitioners: keep migrating to modern, well analyzed algorithms and protocols (TLS 1.3, AEAD, strong randomness), monitor post quantum standardization progress, but distrust absolute claims.
